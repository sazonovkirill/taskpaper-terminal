Navigator and finders

The Navigator🔗

Press Ctrl+G or | to open the Navigator, the list of places to go:

  • INBOX, for things you haven’t sorted yet. The sample’s INBOX project is tagged @inbox, which makes it the inbox; Select Inbox Project in Go to Command makes any project the inbox.
  • The special views: Today, Upcoming, Overdue, Colors, Logbook, Starred and Flagged.
  • Bookmarks, once you have some.
  • Projects, as a tree, each with its number of open tasks.

Use ↓ and ↑ to move and Enter to go there, or type to filter. Type gard, press Enter, and the pane zooms into the garden. Esc closes the Navigator.

Open it again and choose Logbook: what you finished this week, the passports and the sunscreen. Since letters go into the filter, use the arrows here, not j and k.

Bookmarks🔗

Press g h, move to “Website redesign” under “Work” and press b. A bookmark icon appears after its name. Open the Navigator: the project now has an entry of its own under Bookmarks.

Go to Symbol🔗

To find any line at all, press Ctrl+K (or /) for Go to Symbol and type a few letters, such as trm: the match is loose. Enter takes you to the line.

g P does the same over projects alone.

Move to🔗

“Look up tram pass prices” is still in the INBOX, but it belongs with the trip.

Go to it, press g m for Move to, and type before. “Before we go” is at the top of the list. Press Enter. The task moves to the end of that list, and the pane follows it there.

That’s the inbox habit: jot things down in the INBOX, then send each where it belongs.

Press g h to go home. The Reference has the rest of the Navigator’s keys.
