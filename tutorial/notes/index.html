Notes and comments

On this page

Text lines🔗

The simplest note is a line of text, like “Four days in May…” under the trip: no dash, no colon, drawn a little dimmer than tasks.

To add one, press o on a text line, or press Backspace at the start of a new task to delete its dash. Text lines can hold some Markdown: **bold**, `code`, links and # headings.

A note for any line🔗

For more room, any line can have a note attached, of any length, out of sight until you open it.

Go to “Trip to Lisbon” and press \ (backslash). A panel opens over the right half of the pane, with an empty Note and Comments.

Press e (or Enter) to write the note. Type:

Hotel: somewhere in Alfama, with a terrace.

Press Enter for a new line and add “Budget: 120 a night”. Press Esc to save the note.

Comments🔗

Comments are short, dated entries: a running log of what was decided and when.

With the panel still open, press a to add a comment. Type “Sam says May is busy, book soon” and press Enter. tpt stamps it with the date and time.

Press \ again to widen the panel to the whole pane, and once more to close it; Esc closes it at once. The trip’s row now ends in two icons, for the note and for the comments with their count.

You don’t need the panel to add a comment: press c on any line, type the comment in the prompt at the bottom of the screen and press Enter.

In the file🔗

They’re saved as lines under their project, marked @hidden:

Trip to Lisbon:
	Four days in May. The flights are booked, everything else is open.
	NOTE @hidden
		Hotel: somewhere in Alfama, with a terrace.
		Budget: 120 a night
	COMMENTS @hidden
		[2026-04-12 18:27] Sam says May is busy, book soon

Other TaskPaper apps show them as ordinary lines, so nothing is lost. The Reference has the panel’s other keys.
