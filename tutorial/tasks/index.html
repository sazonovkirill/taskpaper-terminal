Tasks and done

On this page

  • Check a task off
  • Hide what’s done
  • Tags you don’t type
  • Star, flag and color

Check a task off🔗

Zoom into “Trip to Lisbon” with ., then move to “Passports” under “Packing” and press x. The dash turns into a check mark ✔, and the task is dimmed and struck through. Do the same on “Sunscreen”. Press x again on a done task to make it open again.

In the file, tpt adds @done with today’s date, as TaskPaper does. Any line can be done: mark a project done and everything inside it dims with it.

Hide what’s done🔗

Press g c: the two packed items disappear. Press it again to bring them back. Nothing is deleted; you only stop seeing them.

Tags you don’t type🔗

tpt stores details as TaskPaper tags, such as @done or @star, but never shows them. You see icons, colors and chips instead, and set them with keys. Typing @ at the start of a word is refused, so your text never turns into a tag by accident; e opens Edit Node, where you can see and change every tag of a line.

Star, flag and color🔗

Try these on the trip:

  • On “Tram 28, early in the morning”, press s to star it. A star icon appears after the text.
  • On “Book the hotel”, press ! to flag it. A red flag appears.
  • On “LX Factory on Sunday”, press m c and then 2 to color it yellow. The whole row is tinted yellow. The picker numbers red, yellow, green and blue from 1 to 4, and C removes the color.

Each mark has a special view listing every such line in the file, as you’ll see in the Navigator.

Press g h to go home. More on tags and done tasks is in the Reference.
