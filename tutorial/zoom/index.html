Zoom and breadcrumbs

On this page

  • Zoom in
  • Zoom out
  • Back and forward
  • Why it matters

Zoom in🔗

Move to “Trip to Lisbon” and press . (period). The screen now shows only the trip, with its name as a title.

The breadcrumb bar at the top now reads ⌂ › Trip to Lisbon, with a house icon for ⌂: the path from the top of the file to here.

Go one level deeper. Move to “Things to see” and press . again. The breadcrumbs grow to ⌂ › Trip to Lisbon › Things to see, and you see three tasks and nothing else.

You can zoom into any line, even a single task, and everything works the same there. You’re still editing the real outline, just one part of it.

Zoom out🔗

Press , (the key beside .) to zoom out one level, back to the trip. Press g h to go all the way home, to the top of the file.

Back and forward🔗

Like a web browser, tpt remembers where you’ve been. Press H to go back, and L to go forward. Alt+← and Alt+→ do the same. Try it: H takes you back into the trip.

With a mouse, you can also click a crumb to zoom there.

Why it matters🔗

Zooming lets one file hold everything without feeling crowded. The trip doesn’t need a list of its own: zoom into it while you plan, and out when you’re done.

Press g h to go home before the next chapter. The other zoom keys are listed in the Reference.
