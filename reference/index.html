Reference

The Reference explains every feature, key and setting of tpt, one topic to a page. The key and settings tables come straight from the app’s source: its command registry and the list in its settings window. They show exactly what the app does.

A few conventions run through every page:

  • Keys are written the way the app writes them in its panels. Ctrl+D means hold Ctrl and press D. gg and Ctrl+W v are sequences: press one key after the other, within a second. A capital letter, such as G, means Shift with that letter.
  • Two columns of keys. Every command has keys that work in any terminal. A few also have a key that only terminals speaking the kitty keyboard protocol can send, such as Ctrl+Enter. Those keys get a column of their own, next to the key that works everywhere.
  • Names. Each command has a name in config.toml. Use it to rebind keys, and every row and setting has a link of its own.
  • A ? next to a command means it also appears in the ? shortcuts panel in the app.

In the app, press ? to see the keys for where you are. Ctrl+P or : opens Go to Command, which lists every command with its keys. For one page with every key, print the cheat sheet.
