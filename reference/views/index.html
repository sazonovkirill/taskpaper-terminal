Special views

On this page

  • Working in a view
  • Today
  • The other views
  • Keys
  • Settings

A special view shows a list of blocks from all over the document in a pane, in place of the outline: everything due today, everything flagged, everything done this week. Each view has its own icon, a Go to key starting with g, and an entry in the Navigator, each after Today, with how many open tasks it lists, or for the Logbook how many completed tasks.

Opening a view is a step in the pane’s history, so H comes back. The pane’s header shows the view’s icon and name in place of the breadcrumbs.

Working in a view🔗

The entries are the blocks themselves, not copies. Each shows its path, dimmed, after its text. Moving, edit mode and block commands work on them as in the outline: done, dates, star, flag, color, Edit Node, selection, delete, duplicate, copy and undo. When an entry stops matching, for example after x in the Overdue view, it leaves the list, except while you are still editing it.

There is no tree around the entries, so commands that need one do nothing in a view: folding, moving to a parent, child or sibling, Zoom Out, adding, grouping or pasting blocks, and indenting. Zoom In on an entry takes you to it in the outline: the pane zooms to its parent and focuses it.

Completed blocks follow the pane’s show completed setting, as in the outline. While they are hidden, a done block leaves the list with every block inside it. The Logbook is the exception.

Today🔗

Today (gt or gT) lists, in document order:

  • every block with a start or due date on or before today,
  • every starred block, while Display starred items in Today view is on, as it is by default.

The list changes at midnight, and whenever the document or the setting changes. The Navigator always lists Today.

Reordering Today🔗

In Today, and only there, Move Up and Move Down (K and J, or Alt+k and Alt+j) reorder the list. The selected entries, or the focused one, move a step past the entry next to them. The order belongs to the app, not the file: the document doesn’t change, nothing is saved to it and there is nothing to undo. Every pane showing Today shares it, and it is kept with the pane layout. Entries you never moved come after the moved ones in document order, so a block that starts matching comes last, and one that leaves the list keeps its place for when it comes back.

The other views🔗

The other views can be grouped. A bold heading above each group names it. Headings are drawn, not blocks: the focus and paging skip them.

ViewGo toListsGrouped by
UpcominggUblocks with a start or due date from tomorrow to a week from today, once each, under the earlier dateday, earliest first: Tomorrow · Thu 8 Oct, Wed 14 Oct
OverduegOopen blocks with a due date before today; done blocks and blocks inside them neverdue date, oldest first: 3 days ago · Sun 4 Oct
ColorsgCblocks with a colorcolor, red to blue
LogbookgLblocks completed from six days ago to today, also inside done blocks, whatever show completed saysday of completion, newest first: Yesterday · Tue 6 Oct
StarredgSstarred blocksnot grouped
FlaggedgFflagged blocksnot grouped

A heading of a day in another year includes the year: Fri 1 Jan 2027. Within a group, entries are in document order. Move Up and Move Down do nothing in these views.
