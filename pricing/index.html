The fine print, honestly

Is tpt really free?
Yes. tpt is open source under the MIT license. Every feature above is in the one and only version, and all three plans are the same app.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no account, no sign-in, no subscription and no telemetry. tpt reads and writes a file on your disk, and that's it.
How do I get it?
cargo install --git https://github.com/sazonovkirill/taskpaper-terminal
Then why is there a pricing page?
Every product website has one. We felt left out.
