## Check a task off

Zoom into "Trip to Lisbon" with <kbd>.</kbd>, then move to "Passports" under "Packing" and press <kbd>x</kbd>. The dash turns into a check mark `✔`, and the task is dimmed and struck through. Do the same on "Sunscreen". Press <kbd>x</kbd> again on a done task to make it open again.

In the file, tpt adds `@done` with today's date, as TaskPaper does. Any line can be done: mark a project done and everything inside it dims with it.

## Hide what's done

Press <kbd>Ctrl+O</kbd> (or <kbd>g</kbd> <kbd>c</kbd>): the two packed items disappear. Press it again to bring them back. Nothing is deleted; you only stop seeing them.

## Tags you don't type

tpt stores details as TaskPaper tags, such as `@done` or `@star`, but never shows them. You see icons, colors and chips instead, and set them with keys. Typing `@` at the start of a word is refused, so your text never turns into a tag by accident; <kbd>e</kbd> opens Edit Node, where you can see and change every tag of a line.

## Star, flag and color

Try these on the trip:

- On "Tram 28, early in the morning", press <kbd>*</kbd> to **star** it. A star icon appears after the text.
- On "Book the hotel", press <kbd>!</kbd> to **flag** it. A red flag appears.
- On "LX Factory on Sunday", press <kbd>c</kbd> <kbd>3</kbd> to color it yellow. The whole row is tinted yellow. <kbd>c</kbd> <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>c</kbd> <kbd>6</kbd> pick red, orange, yellow, green, blue and violet, and <kbd>C</kbd> removes the color.
